## 2024-05-18 - [Persian Localization Layout]
**Learning:** Proper right-to-left layout requires the `dir="rtl"` attribute and `lang="fa"` on the HTML element. This is crucial for semantic parsing, text rendering, and screen reader support on Persian localized sites. Without this base structure, localized content becomes inaccessible and visually broken.
**Action:** Always verify `lang` and `dir` on HTML roots when starting localized projects or evaluating new templates.
